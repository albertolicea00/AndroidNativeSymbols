# Privacy Policy — Native Symbols

**Effective date: October 15, 2026**

Native Symbols is an offline reference app for browsing Android system drawables and classic Material icons.

## Information collection and sharing

Native Symbols does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information. The app does not require an account and currently does not include advertising, analytics, or other third-party SDKs that collect information.

## Local preferences and clipboard

The app stores the selected grid or list layout preference locally on your device. This preference is removed when you uninstall the app.

When you tap a Copy action, the selected code snippet is written to the Android system clipboard so you can paste it into another app. Native Symbols does not read clipboard contents or transmit them. The Android operating system or another app may manage clipboard history according to its own behavior and settings.

## Network access

The icon catalog is bundled with the app or provided by Android. Native Symbols does not need an internet connection to browse or copy icons.

## Children's privacy

Native Symbols is a developer reference utility and is not designed specifically for children. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, this policy will be updated to describe those changes. The effective date above will also be updated.
